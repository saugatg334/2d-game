About

About Nepali Racer

Nepali Racer is a browser-built 2D hill-climbing racing game inspired by the roads, culture and landscapes of Nepal. Ride the Kathmandu Valley, push across the East-West Highway, and climb towards the high Himalaya — tuning your chosen vehicle, unlocking regional riders and mastering each stage. It runs entirely in your browser with no downloads, and your progress is saved locally on your device.
